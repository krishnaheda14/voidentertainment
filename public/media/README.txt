DROP YOUR PHOTOS AND VIDEOS HERE
================================

venues/   -> one photo per venue. Exact filenames the site looks for:
             mercii.jpg
             baglami.jpg
             onrique.jpg
             bastian.jpg
             Recommended: 1600x1200 or larger, JPG, under 400 KB each.

gallery/  -> poster stills for the "Past nights" wall:
             nye-bastian.jpg
             onrique-012.jpg
             baglami-bolly.jpg
             mercii-low.jpg
             holi-bastian.jpg
             Recommended: 1600x1000, JPG, under 400 KB each.

videos/   -> aftermovie MP4s if you are self-hosting them.
             Keep each file under ~15 MB or use YouTube instead
             (set `youtube: 'VIDEO_ID'` in src/data/site.js).

You do NOT have to add any of these to launch. Missing files fall back to a
generated gradient tile labelled "Photo pending" — nothing breaks.
